Information we collect

We collect information you provide when you contact us, make a booking, create a guest account, or communicate with us. This may include your name, email address, telephone number, country code, stay dates, number of guests, booking notes, and messages.

Our payment provider processes card and payment information. Mistwood Residence does not store full card numbers or card security codes on this website.

How we use your information

We use your information to respond to enquiries, confirm and manage reservations, process payments, provide guest support, send essential booking communications, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

With your permission where required, we may also send occasional information about stays or offers. You may opt out of promotional messages at any time.

Sharing, retention & security

We share only the information needed with trusted service providers that help us run the property and website, such as our payment processor, email provider, booking tools, and professional advisers. We do not sell personal information.

We retain booking and communication records for as long as reasonably necessary for guest service, accounting, legal, and operational purposes. We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Your choices
